# Rovelle

Rovelle is Mira's hometown: a busy port city built between a headland and two rivers. Beyond it lies the Grey Sea, whose subsurface currents can be difficult to infer from calm surface water alone. The city records tides, checks instruments, and sometimes reaches a dangerous certainty too early.

It is neither a magical ring-gate metropolis nor a covert technical facility. Its residents include pilots, surveyors, civil servants, traders, fishmongers, families, and children. Their needs do not exist merely to serve Mira's plot.

The city's important divisions include Cape Ward, the Inner Harbor, the older salt docks and other waterfront districts. Its politics involve public safety, trade, access, evidence, and the uneven costs of risk.
