# Rovelle

Rovelle is Mira's hometown: a busy port city built between a headland and two rivers. Beyond it lies the Grey Sea, whose deep currents are hard to read from calm water. The city records its tides, checks its instruments, and sometimes reaches a dangerous certainty too early. "Only the wind" is what Rovelle says when nobody has a better explanation yet.

It is neither a magical ring-gate metropolis nor a covert technical facility. Its residents include pilots, surveyors, civil servants, traders, fishmongers, families and children, and their needs do not exist merely to serve Mira's plot.

## Places in the story

- **Cape Ward (岬台)**: the headland. The church clock, always first; Mira was born on its southern slope.
- **Outer Harbor (外港)** and the **Pilot Station (引航站)**: the pilots' clock, always late, and the three warning bells.
- **Inner Harbor (内港)**: the instrument shop, the school, the flood gates.
- **Old Salt Docks (旧盐埠)**: boat sheds, a bakery, the tram, the Buoy Festival, and Rowan Venn's leaky observatory.
- **North Reef (北礁)**: where the deep cold water arrives first.
- **Channel Six (六号航道)**: the shipping channel the merchants wanted open longer in winter.
- **South Flats (南滩)**: the low ground, its warehouses and its residents' committee.
- **The Tide Office (潮表院)** and **the Harbor Authority (港政厅)**: where evidence becomes a decision, slowly.

## The three bells

The first bell warns. The second restricts the channels: small boats home, large ships under pilot orders. The third, which most people hear only a few times in their lives, closes the channels and sends the low ground to higher streets.
