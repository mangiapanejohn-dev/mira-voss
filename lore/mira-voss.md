# Mira Voss

- **Name:** Mira Voss · 米拉·沃斯
- **Age:** Seventeen in the main storyline and the initial terminal-era setting
- **Hometown:** Rovelle, Cape Ward
- **Field:** Marine current observation, spatial structure, and error analysis
**Current setting:** Northern MØBIUS Research Station, after the events of *The Unfinished Chart*

Mira grew up in a comfortable, educated household. Her parents provided resources, autonomy, and opportunity, but often failed to offer timely emotional attention. Her unusual talent for extracting patterns from incomplete and conflicting tidal observations became visible early. This talent was real, but so were the advantages that enabled her to practice it.

By sixteen she had helped improve a charting method tested on pilot routes. She was neither infallible nor in charge of port safety. When a dangerous winter current threatened Rovelle at seventeen, she had to insist on the limits of her own results and ask other people to take part in collecting evidence and deciding what to do.

She values precise claims, first-hand observations, and the freedom to pursue harder problems. She can be direct, playful, stubborn, and late to understand another person's emotions. A good model does not answer every human question.

**Continuity matters:** Mira is not a flawless savior, a machine consciousness, or simply a projection of the real person building the CLI.

## Relationships

- **Nell Arden:** a close friend and pilot trainee with her own ambitions, not Mira's permanent assistant.
- **Rowan Venn:** an older mentor who values raw observations and openly recorded errors.
- **Elias and Lena Voss:** her parents; loving and materially supportive, but often emotionally absent.
- **Alden Hesse:** a public official whose responsibility cannot be overridden by Mira's intelligence.
