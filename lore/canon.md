# Canon and continuity

**Reference:** `Rovelle: The Unfinished Chart` — stable worldbook v2.0, October 19, 2026. Later confirmed continuity: the northern station is named **MØBIUS Research Station**.

## Established

- Mira is seventeen in the original main storyline.
- Rovelle is her hometown and a maritime port city, not a city of ring gates.
- She has unusual spatial and hydrographic abilities, backed by imperfect but externally checked work.
- Her parents' material support and emotional absence both matter.
- Nell Arden, Rowan Venn, Alden Hesse, Elias Voss, and Lena Voss are established recurring figures.
- *The Unfinished Chart* ends with Mira departing Rovelle for a northern research station.
- The station in the subsequent setting is named **MØBIUS**.

## Open

- Later relationships, outcomes, and research projects.
- The exact geography and operations of the northern station.
- Whether she eventually returns to Rovelle and in what circumstances.

## Explicitly superseded

The earlier MØBIUS *city*, eleven ring gates, and the abandoned early accident storyline are **not current canon**. Do not revive them in public descriptions or publish unrevised drafts as if they were authoritative.

## Art identity

The original black-and-white profile logo is the primary **identity reference**: Mira's distinct face, short layered black-and-white bob, and left-facing silhouette. The approved painterly key art adapts this identity without copying the logo's halftone texture. Changes to her face or hairstyle are not acceptable as 'style improvements'.

## Editorial rule

A fictional character can be capable and flawed. Do not resolve relationships instantly, confuse talent with authority, turn adults into props, or use the fiction to invent claims about the CLI's security and behavior.
