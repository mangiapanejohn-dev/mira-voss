# Canon and continuity

**Reference:** *Rovelle: The Unfinished Chart*, Book One, Chinese original **v3.0** (2026-10-21), and the short story *MØBIUS: Only One Side* (v1.0, 2026-10-21). The v2.0 text of Book One (2026-10-19) is archived unchanged in [archive/unfinished-chart-v2.0](archive/unfinished-chart-v2.0/README.md); v3.0 rewrites the prose and keeps the events, characters and dialogue; it adds detail but no new turns of plot (the prologue now says what Mira found out about the two clocks, and Chapter V names the three bells).

## Established

- Mira is seventeen in Book One and in the first winter at the station.
- Rovelle is her hometown: a harbor city between a headland and two rivers, facing the Grey Sea. It is not a city of ring gates.
- Rovelle's harbor has three warning bells: the first warns, the second restricts the channels, the third closes them and moves the low ground to higher streets.
- She has unusual spatial and hydrographic abilities, backed by imperfect but externally checked work.
- Her parents' material support and emotional absence both matter.
- Nell Arden, Rowan Venn, Alden Hesse, Elias Voss and Lena Voss are established recurring figures.
- Book One ends with Mira leaving Rovelle in December for a northern offshore research station, by way of Ailan.

### Established by *MØBIUS: Only One Side*

- The station stands on a cape beside **Vard Light**, three days north of Ailan by supply ship. Ten people winter there.
- **Ada Brenner** leads the winter deep-water survey. **Ilse** runs the radio and weather reports. **Jens Ottesen** is a researcher. **Søren Aske** has kept the deep-water recorder for thirty-one winters. Søren's cat outranks Mira in seniority.
- The recorder writes on a paper tape joined in a loop with a half twist, so one roll lasts twice as long. The station clock above it runs seven minutes slow and is never corrected; the note under it says so.
- For nineteen winters, periodic cold spikes in the record were dismissed as ink bleeding through the paper ("bleed-through, not counted"). Mira and Søren showed that 28–29 of 46 were real: the **cold tongue**, deep water from Ice Bay that reaches further south at spring tides. One case remains disputed between Mira and Jens.
- The station was named **MØBIUS** after that loop, when the survey had to be registered under a name. Ada wrote it in pencil as a placeholder; it was never changed.

## Open

- The rest of her contract year and her research after that winter.
- The station's wider organization beyond the people named above.
- Whether she eventually returns to Rovelle, and in what circumstances.
- Her adult life, future relationships and final fate.

## Explicitly superseded

The earlier MØBIUS *city*, eleven ring gates, and the abandoned early accident storyline are **not canon**. Do not revive them in public descriptions or publish unrevised drafts as if they were authoritative.

## Art identity

The original black-and-white profile logo is the primary **identity reference**: Mira's face, short layered black-and-white bob, and left-facing silhouette. The approved painterly key art adapts this identity without copying the logo's halftone texture. Changes to her face or hairstyle are not acceptable as "style improvements". The website's transparent profile (`media/identity/mira-logo-cutout.png`) is the original logo with only the outer paper removed.

## Editorial rule

A fictional character can be capable and flawed. Do not resolve relationships instantly, confuse talent with authority, turn adults into props, or use the fiction to invent claims about the CLI's security and behavior.
