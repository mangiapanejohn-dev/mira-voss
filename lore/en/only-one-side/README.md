# MØBIUS: Only One Side

**Mira Voss · MØBIUS short story · The first winter · English translation of the Chinese original · v1.0 · 2026-10-21**

It begins where Book One of *Rovelle: The Unfinished Chart* ends. Mira reaches an offshore research station in the north, and in a roll of recording tape twisted half a turn she finds the stretch of sea that nineteen years of notes had written off as *bleed-through, not counted*. The station's name, **MØBIUS**, begins that winter too.
