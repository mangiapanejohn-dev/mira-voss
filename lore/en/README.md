# Rovelle: The Unfinished Chart

**Mira Voss / Rovelle · Book One · English translation of the Chinese original · v3.0 · 2026-10-21**

The story takes place in the harbor city of Rovelle. At its end Mira leaves home for an offshore research station in the north; the station is later named **MØBIUS**, and how it got that name is told in the short story [*MØBIUS: Only One Side*](only-one-side/README.md).

## About this translation

The Chinese text in [lore/zh](../zh/README.md) is the original; where the two differ, it is the authority. The translation follows it chapter by chapter, with nothing added or left out.

The story, its people and its places are fictional, and say nothing about what the actual CLI can do.
