# The Unfinished Chart

*Original Chinese title: 《罗维尔：未完成的海图》 · Rovelle, Book One · v3.0*

> **Spoiler-free introduction**

Rovelle is a harbor city built around the dangerous difference between what the sea looks like and what its instruments can establish. Mira Voss grew up measuring that difference. At seventeen, with an invitation from the north waiting for her, she discovers that her research may be used outside the conditions in which it was tested.

To correct the record, she must do something harder than build another accurate model: make its limits public, ask others to verify them, and accept that a decision that falls on many people cannot belong to her alone.

Along the way, friendships and family ties she has treated as settled begin to show what she has failed to notice. The book ends not with a perfect reconciliation, but with a departure toward the northern station later named MØBIUS.

**Read it:** [online, chapter by chapter](https://mangiapanejohn-dev.github.io/mira-voss/read.html?work=unfinished-chart), or the [Chinese original in this repository](zh/README.md). The story continues in [*MØBIUS: Only One Side*](zh/only-one-side/README.md).
