# The Unfinished Chart

*Original Chinese title: 《未完成的海图》 · Rovelle, Book One*

> **Spoiler-free introduction**

Rovelle is a harbor city built around the dangerous difference between what the sea looks like and what its instruments can establish. Mira Voss grew up measuring those differences. At seventeen, with an invitation from the north waiting for her, she discovers that her research may be used outside the conditions in which it was tested.

To correct the record, she must do something more difficult than producing another accurate model: make the limitations public, invite others to verify them, and accept that a decision involving many people cannot belong to her alone.

Along the way, friendships and family relationships she has treated as stable begin to reveal what she has failed to notice. The first book ends not with a perfect reconciliation, but with a departure toward the northern research station later named MØBIUS.
