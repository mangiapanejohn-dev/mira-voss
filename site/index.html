The story · Book I

The Unfinished Chart.

A map can be accurate and still leave something vital out.

At seventeen, Mira Voss is a gifted researcher in the harbor city of Rovelle. When her work is used beyond the conditions she tested, she must make its limits public and invite others to check what she found. The cost reaches beyond the harbor: a friendship, a family, and the belief that she can solve everything alone.

Her first journey ends with a departure for the northern research station later named MØBIUS. The original Chinese novella is preserved in full, from prologue to final chapter.

The product

A presence
in the terminal.

Mira Voss is a proprietary AI agent distributed through npm. Start her in the directory where you work; the published CLI can read files, edit, run commands, and report what it has done. Actual behavior depends on the installed release, selected model, configuration, and permissions.

Installation guide ↗
INSTALL MIRA VOSSnpm · v1.0.0
$ npm install -g mira-voss

Node.js 22 or newer. Run mira in your working directory; mobius is an alternate command.
