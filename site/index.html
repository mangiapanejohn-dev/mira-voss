THE CHARACTER

The city trusted her chart.
She knew its limits.

At seventeen, Mira Voss can find patterns in incomplete tidal observations that others miss. When her work is used to argue for a dangerous extension of Rovelle's shipping season, she has to explain exactly where her findings stop.

The harbor crisis asks more of her than a better calculation. Mira must let other people test her work, make their own decisions, and carry their own part of the story.

THE TERMINAL AGENT

Mira, at work.

Mira Voss is also a proprietary AI agent distributed through npm. Start the CLI in the directory where you work. The published release can read files, edit, run commands, and show what it has done, subject to its configuration and permissions.

This GitHub repository publishes documentation, the story, and character art. It does not contain the private runtime.
